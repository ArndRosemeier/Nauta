# Gate G1: the sea, judged by Arnd on the iPad

Everything of milestone N1 is built. This gate is Arnd's: does the sea convince, does it run, and do the budgets stand. About ten minutes. Tap each link on the iPad (or in the home-screen app); tap the view once so the sound starts, and drag to look around.

## 1. Look

You stand 4.5 m over the water, where the brig's quarterdeck is. For each scene: turn all the way round, look into the sun or moon and away from it, look straight down and out to the horizon.

Also once from the masthead, 30 m up: [breeze at noon](https://arndrosemeier.github.io/Nauta/?sea=breeze&hour=12&eye=30), and once from under water: [three metres down](https://arndrosemeier.github.io/Nauta/?sea=breeze&hour=12&eye=-3).

What to judge:

- **Is it better than MasterWorld's sea**, side by side with MasterWorld open on the PC or in a second tab? This is the gate's main question.
- **The waves.** Do the heights look right for a calm, a breeze and a gale? Does the calm still breathe (the long swell)? Does anything repeat, march in rows or swim under you?
- **The surface near you.** Small waves and ripples down to your feet, no flat or blurred patch, no weave pattern.
- **The horizon.** A clean line, no flicker or sparkle noise far out, no visible steps where the sea's detail changes.
- **Light.** The sun's and the moon's glitter path, the sky in the water, the colour of deep water, light through the crests toward the sun.
- **Foam.** White water where waves break in the breeze and the gale, lingering and fading; none in the calm.
- **Gusts.** Dark patches travelling over the water in the breeze.

## 2. Listen

Same scenes, with the sound on. Eyes shut: can you tell the calm from the breeze from the gale? Does the sound belong to the picture (a crest passing near you is heard near you)? Does anything loop noticeably within a minute or two?

## 3. Measure

Open [the bench in a breeze](https://arndrosemeier.github.io/Nauta/?bench) and [the bench in a gale at dusk](https://arndrosemeier.github.io/Nauta/?bench&sea=gale&hour=17.7). Each flies for a minute and then shows its numbers. Take a screenshot of each result and paste both into the design thread. They say whether the sea keeps 60 frames a second on the iPad and what it costs.

Then open the game plainly with [the HUD](https://arndrosemeier.github.io/Nauta/?hud): its second line names the quality level the iPad settled on (it starts at high and steps down only if frames come late). Tell the design thread which level it shows after ten seconds.

## 4. Confirm the budgets

The budgets in [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md#budgets) are proposals until this gate. The ones that matter to you as the player: 60 frames a second, steady; at most 15 s from tap to standing on deck; at most 15 MB before the title shows and 60 MB for a whole duel. Say "budgets OK" or name what you want different.

## What to send back

One message is enough: better than MasterWorld or not, anything that looked or sounded wrong (with the scene), the two bench screenshots, the quality level, and "budgets OK". Anything that does not convince goes back into N1 before N2 is called finished.
