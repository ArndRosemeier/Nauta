Sail her (?sail) and she now leaves a wake: waves spreading behind her in a wedge, foam and a smooth slick that stay on the water where she passed.
Her bow wave climbs her stem, foam hugs her waterline as she pitches and rolls, her side stays wet where the sea climbed it and drains, and spray flies over her bow when she slams into a sea.
