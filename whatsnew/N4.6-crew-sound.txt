The crew is heard at work: give an order with the order buttons and the boatswain's pipe shrills its own call for it (each kind of order has a different one), then bare feet pound across the deck and the gangs haul in rhythm at the ropes.
When she comes round after a tack the pipe calls "haul away" and the yards are swung; a missed tack gets one long shrill note, and finished work a short "carry on".
The ship's bell strikes every five minutes of play, one bell more each time up to eight; below deck all of it comes muffled through the planking. Nobody speaks: no voices, only pipe, feet, ropes and bell.
