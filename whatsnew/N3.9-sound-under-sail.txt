The sails are heard: the wind sings in the rigging (louder close-hauled, quieter running before it), loose canvas flogs while a sail is let fall or shivers, a sail fills with a whump and slams when it goes aback, blocks squeal as the lines run and the yards creak as they are braced round.
Try it on ?sail: tap once, give "all sail", then "tack", and listen with your eyes shut. Below deck it all comes muffled through the planking.
