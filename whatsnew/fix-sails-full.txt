Full and by now means what a seaman means: the helmsman steers by the sails, as close to the wind as every set sail stays full, so no sail shivers or lies aback while she sails.
Under plain sail she lies a little further off the wind than under battle sail, but goes faster, so she gets to windward about as well.
Try it: order "full and by" and watch the sails; they only flap while she tacks or when a mast is laid aback.
