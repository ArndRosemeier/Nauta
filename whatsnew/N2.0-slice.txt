A first piece of the brig, 7 m of her waist built to full quality: deck and beams, bulwark with two carronades run out, the main shrouds with deadeyes and ratlines, the pin rail with coiled lines, the hatch with its grating, the berth deck below with a lantern. Open the game with ?slice at the end of the address.
The buttons at the bottom take you to the waist, behind a gun, up by the shrouds, below deck, into the main top, or into a boat alongside; drag to look round. She rides the waves as the sea moves her.
