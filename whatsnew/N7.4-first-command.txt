The main screen has "Your first command": your first lieutenant shows you in speech bubbles how to look about, walk, use the glass, make sail, tack, steer for her, beat to quarters and give the word to fire, against a green captain who waits for you.
The man to tap has a brass ring, and the orders he wants are lit in brass in the fan. "Skip the lessons" (top right) ends them and the fight goes on.
