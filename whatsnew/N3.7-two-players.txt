Two captains can now sail in one match: open the game with ?sail and tap "Two players" at the top left, open a room on one device, and on the other tap the room in the list (or open the link the room shows). Each of you commands your own brig.
The match starts with the two brigs 1,500 m apart, the wind on the beam, both closing; you see the other ship where she really is, in the same waves.
If the device that opened the room leaves, the other one carries the match on by itself.
