The other brig is drawn whole: hull, rig, guns and boats on her deck, and her sails and yards as her captain has them set and braced, from far on the horizon to close aboard without a jump.
The spyglass: tap Glass (above the version button) or spread two fingers on the view to raise it; spread or pinch to draw it out from 4 to 20 times, pinch right in to lower it. It sways a little with your hands and with her roll.
Try it with ?sail: she starts about 1,500 m off; find her, raise the glass and read how she is sailing.
