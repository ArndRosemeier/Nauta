Shot holes below the waterline now let the sea in and the pumps fight it: she settles and lists toward the holes. Masts and spars come down when they are shot through or their shrouds are cut with the wind on them, and they drag alongside.
A ball fired close can set her afire, a ball through the wheel leaves her rudder trailing, and fire left to burn reaches the magazine. None of this is drawn yet; in ?sail you may feel her slow or stop answering the helm.
