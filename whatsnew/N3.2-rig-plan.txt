Behind the scenes: the brig's whole rig now exists as a plan: both masts with their yards, the bowsprit, the boom and gaff, eleven sails and 190 ropes, each running rope on its own belaying pin.
The tops, crosstrees, every yard's footropes and the bowsprit are joined to the decks, so the captain can later climb to all of them; nothing new to see in the game yet.
