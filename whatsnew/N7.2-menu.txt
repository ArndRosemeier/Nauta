The plain address is now the game: the title plays while it loads, one tap begins, and the menu sits over her quarterdeck at sea.
Fight alone against a green, seasoned or master captain, host or join a duel, or walk the ship; choose wind, hour, how you meet and the length, or just tap "Beat to quarters".
In a match the compass rose opens the captain's pause (alone the fight stands still); when the fight ends a card says how, with "Again" and "Back". Dimmed parts are still to come.
