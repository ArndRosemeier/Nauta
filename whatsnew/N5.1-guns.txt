Behind the scenes: the brig's 16 carronades and 2 long sixes now go through their drill: sponge, load, ram, run out, fire, recoil.
A carronade with all her men reloads in 35 seconds, a long six in 30; with men missing it takes longer, and with fewer than two nothing happens.
You cannot see or fire the guns yet; the orders to fire and the drill drawn come next.
