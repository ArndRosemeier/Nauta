Behind the scenes: shot now flies. Round shot, chain and grape leave the gun as the ship lies at that instant, so firing on the up-roll sends it high and on the down-roll low, and round shot skips off the water.
Fired as they bear in a moderate sea, about three shots in four strike an enemy brig at 100 m, under half at 200 m and one in four at 400 m.
You cannot see or fire the guns yet; the orders to fire and the drawing come next.
