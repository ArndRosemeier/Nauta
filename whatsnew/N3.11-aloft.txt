The captain can go aloft: on the walk page, stand at the shrouds by her rail and push outward to get on them, then push up or down to climb. Keep climbing past the futtock shrouds and you go over the rim onto the top.
On a top, look up and walk to its edge to climb the topmast shrouds to the crosstrees and look down on your deck. Walk to the edge without looking up to go back down. Level with a yard, push sideways to step onto its footrope, and sidle out to the yardarm.
Forward, walk into the bowsprit beside its heel to get up on it, walk out along it, and keep going onto the jib-boom. In a hard roll he stops and holds on.
