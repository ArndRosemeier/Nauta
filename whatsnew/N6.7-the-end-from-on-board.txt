The end of a fight is now seen: a ship that strikes has her colours hauled down, a ship that sinks, burns out or blows up goes down in front of you, and your own sinking brings the sea over your deck and takes you under.
After it the end card waits until the ending has played out, then offers "Again" and "Back". The guns fall silent when the fight is over.
