The officers answer your orders in speech bubbles: open ?sail, give "Tack" and the master says "Aye aye, sir. Ready about!" while the boatswain passes it down.
An order she cannot take is answered with why ("She has no way on her, sir"), and the lookout, master, gunner and carpenter report on their own: a sail sighted, her ports opening, guns bearing, water rising.
In English or German, as the settings choose. Until the officers are drawn, the bubbles hang where each man stands.
