Damage is heard: a ball smashing into her timber close by is a splintering crash where it struck (and you are jolted), far off a dull thud after the flash; splinters fly, a struck gun clangs, cut rigging twangs, sails tear, a mast cracks, groans and crashes over with its rigging parting.
Fires crackle where they burn, below deck the sea rushes in at the holes under the waterline, and a sinking ship groans and breaks up; "hands to the pumps", "fire party" and "clear the wreckage" have their own calls.
