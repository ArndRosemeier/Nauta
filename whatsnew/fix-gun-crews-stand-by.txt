Until you beat to quarters, the gun crews stand by in the waist, on the forecastle and below at their messes, so the quarterdeck is open: only the wheel, the officers and the sentries are on it. Beat to quarters and watch them run to their guns, which takes about half a minute; orders that need the men on the quarterdeck braces take a few seconds longer too.
Stand on the quarterdeck, then give "Beat to quarters" and look down the deck.
