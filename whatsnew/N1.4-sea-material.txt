The sea has its own look: it mirrors the sky and clouds in a calm, the sun and moon draw a glittering path on it, deep water is dark blue, and light shines through the tops of crests.
Whitecaps break on the crests from a fresh breeze on, and their foam lingers and fades behind the wave; gusts drift over the water as darker ruffled patches.
