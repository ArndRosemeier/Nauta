You can sail her now: open the game with ?sail and the brig is under way, with an Orders panel in the top right corner. Tap a compass point to steer there, or tap tack, wear, heave to, full and by, a point up or away, battle, plain or all sail, back a topsail, fill. You can also type an order ("steer 090", "ready about").
The panel shows her heading, speed, the wind and her point of sail, what the crew is doing and how long it will take, and every answer: "wearing round, sir", "she has no way on her", done. A grey button is one she would refuse now; a gold one is the order standing.
The yards swing round as the crew braces them, but the sails are not drawn yet, so watch the panel, the yards and the sea going by, or tap "50 m off" at the bottom to watch her turn from beside her. Tap "Orders" to fold the panel away.
