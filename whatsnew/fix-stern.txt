The back of the ship is built: from the quarterdeck you now look aft at her stern, red inside with two stern ports and the taffrail across the top, instead of open sea.
Outside, her counter sits lower, as on the original plans, under a stern board with its moulding, two ports and a quarter piece at each corner, and her rudder hangs on the sternpost and turns as the helmsman steers.
