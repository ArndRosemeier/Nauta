The guns now move: at quarters the port lids swing up and the guns run out; when they fire you see the flash and a puff at the muzzle, the gun leaps back on its ropes, and the ball flies.
Shot that falls short throws up a splash and leaves white water on the sea; shot that strikes a ship throws a burst of splinters. The other brig's guns work the same way.
Try it under sail: beat to quarters, hold for my word, then fire when she is abeam. Watch from the Gun standpoint, or through the glass to see where the shot lands.
