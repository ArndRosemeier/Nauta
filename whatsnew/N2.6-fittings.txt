The brig is furnished: long sixes with their shot, sponges and tubs by every gun, the wheel and binnacle, capstan, pumps, bitts and fife rails, the bell, two boats on the gallows and the gig at the stern, the anchors at the bows, hammock nettings on the rails.
Below deck there are mess tables, cots and sea chests, the galley stove, and water casks in the hold with a passage between them. Open the page with ?ship and try the standpoint buttons.
