The guns are heard: each of her own guns a deafening crack and boom with the deck jolting under you, a broadside rippling down her side and leaving the world dull and your ears ringing for a few seconds; below deck her guns thump through the timbers. The enemy's guns come after their flash by the speed of sound, a heavy boom far off, and her shot tears past overhead and splashes into the sea around you.
